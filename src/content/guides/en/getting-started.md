Grok Bot gives you persistent AI teammates: each Bot has a name, a job, and context that builds up over time. Every Bot works on a cloud computer with a browser, files, and a terminal, so it finishes work inside your real tools instead of handing back drafts.

## Before you begin

You need an eligible plan: Premium, Super, or Ultra on The X Subscription, or a Cursor plan that includes Grok Bot. You can also link a SuperGrok or X Premium+ subscription.

## 1. Install the app

The desktop app runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux. Download it from [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot) or from the Grok Bot page of the Cursor dashboard. Phones get it from the App Store or Google Play.

## 2. Sign in

Choose **Sign in** on the welcome screen and finish in your browser. Grok Bot uses your Cursor account, so there is no separate login. If your access comes from a Grok Business seat, choose **Sign in with Grok**.

## 3. Meet your first Bot

After a short intro, Grok Bot creates your first Bot — named **Grok Bot** — and opens a chat. To add more, click **+** in the sidebar and choose **Create a Bot**.

Give each Bot **one primary job** in its first message. A focused Bot builds more useful context than a catch-all helper. See [Bots](/en/bots/) for ready-made jobs.

## 4. Give it a first task

Type in the composer or dictate (`Cmd/Ctrl+D` on desktop). A strong request covers five things:

- the outcome you want
- the sources to use
- constraints
- the deliverable
- where the Bot should stop and check with you

For a quick first win, attach a document and ask for a five-bullet summary, adding "Don't change the source file." There is a reusable [request template](/en/prompts/request-template/).

## 5. Sign-ins for other tools

When a Bot needs to log into an app, it uses a login saved in Vault or a connected password manager, or asks you through a Secure Form. The Bot never sees the password. For SSO, passkeys, or anything a form can't handle, click **Open computer**, finish the step, and hand control back.

## 6. Review and reuse

Ask for revisions and state lasting preferences explicitly. Once a process is stable, save it as a [skill or routine](/en/guides/skills-and-routines/). Before letting a Bot change external systems, read the Approvals and Auto Review section of the official security guide.

> If something breaks, fully quit and reopen the app. That clears most setup problems.
