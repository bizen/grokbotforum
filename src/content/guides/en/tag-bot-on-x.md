Tag **@bot** in a post or reply on X and your main Bot receives the task. The work and its results stay in that Bot's conversation in the Grok Bot app; on X, @bot only posts a confirmation.

## Setup

1. Connect your X account on grok.com under **Settings → Account**.
2. Get Grok Bot access on the same Grok account — install the app from [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot) or link a SuperGrok subscription.
3. Create at least one Bot of your own. Bots shared with you can't take tagged requests.

The first time, X tells you that tagged requests start work automatically and use your Grok Bot usage.

## What your Bot receives

- the post you tagged it in
- the post you replied to
- any quoted post, including still photos

## What @bot's reply means

| Reply | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A confirmation line | Your Bot has the task |
| A sign-up link | No Grok account is connected to the posting X account |
| "Grok Bot isn't set up yet" | Accounts are connected, but you haven't created a Bot |

## Availability

Not yet available for accounts based in Australia, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and a few other regions. Eligibility follows the country X has on record for your account — your current location and VPNs don't matter.

## If @bot doesn't reply

No reply means your Bot didn't get the request. Common causes:

- your account's region isn't supported
- you replied in a thread that already includes @bot without tagging it yourself
- the post also tags @grok
- the post, its parent, or a quoted post contains a video or GIF
- the post failed moderation
- Grok Bot isn't linked to your Grok account, or your weekly usage is used up

## Give your Bot more of X

The optional **X for Grok Bot** plugin lets your Bot search posts, read timelines, check mentions, and use trends and bookmarks. Tagged requests reach your Bot with or without it.
